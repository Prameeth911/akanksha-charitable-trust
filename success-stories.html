Stories of Change

Real impact is best understood through the small, daily triumphs of children and educators in our partner classrooms.

Student Journey

From Hesitation to Participation

In a higher primary school near Puttur, 8-year-old Ananya rarely raised her hand during reading periods. Struggling to combine Kannada compound letters, she preferred to remain silent in the back row.

When the Grantha Mitra phonics flashcards and buddy reading circles were introduced, Ananya received gentle, non-judgmental guidance from a trained local volunteer. Within four months, she progressed from recognizing single syllables to reading short folktales aloud to her classmates with proud smiles.

School Evolution

A Classroom Becomes a Reading Space

Government School Bettampady had a locked cupboard with old encyclopedias that children seldom opened. With support from Aakanksha's School Library Development project, the space was transformed.

Low-height wooden display racks, colorful floor mats, and over 150 graded illustrated storybooks turned the room into a vibrant reading sanctuary. Students formed their own library committee to issue and care for books during recess.

STEM Experience

Learning Through Doing

During the annual Mini Science Mela, 6th-grade student Karthik had never built an experiment outside his textbook drawings. Using low-cost items—a plastic syringe, tubing, and cardboard—he constructed a working hydraulic lift model.

Explaining Pascal’s principle to visiting parents and teachers boosted his confidence tremendously. Karthik now leads his school's after-school science observation club with zeal.
