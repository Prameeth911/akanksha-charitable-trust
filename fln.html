Flagship Educational Initiative

Strengthening Foundational Literacy & Numeracy

The Grantha Mitra FLN initiative empowers early grade learners with essential reading, comprehension, and mathematical problem-solving skills.

About Grantha Mitra

Bridging Learning Gaps in Early Childhood Education

Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (FLN) is the bedrock upon which all future learning rests. If a child cannot read fluently or solve basic arithmetic by grade 3, subsequent schooling becomes difficult.

Grantha Mitra operates as an intensive, joyful intervention within government and community primary schools in and around Puttur, Karnataka. We equip teachers and volunteers with scaffolded learning kits, graded Kannada and English reading materials, and interactive math manipulatives.

By shifting from rote memorization to meaningful reading and experiential computation, we help every child find joy and self-confidence in their learning journey.

The Grantha Mitra Learning Cycle

Read • Understand • Calculate • Apply

From decoding sounds to fluent comprehension; from counting objects to solving daily life word problems.

Key Pillars

How Grantha Mitra Works on the Ground

A multi-layered pedagogical framework that supports both child and teacher.

👩‍🏫

Teacher-Led Learning Sessions

Co-designed lesson plans that enable teachers to conduct engaging 45-minute daily FLN hours with interactive storytelling and group activities.

📖

Literacy Practice

Phonics-based letter recognition, sight word cards, graded story cards, and guided reading circles in Kannada and basic English.

🔢

Numeracy Practice

Concrete-to-Abstract math training using counting beads, place-value sticks, number charts, and practical everyday calculation games.

📋

Learner-Level Assessment

Periodic baseline, midline, and endline competency assessments that identify specific learning gaps without causing exam stress.

🌱

Remedial Support

Dedicated volunteer-led small group sessions for children who need extra time and personalized guidance to catch up with their peers.

🤝

School & Community Engagement

Regular parent orientation days and SDMC meetings to share child progress portfolios and encourage home-based reading habits.
