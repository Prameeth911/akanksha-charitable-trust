Community Action

Be Part of the Change

Whether you have two hours on weekends or can lead weekly reading clubs, your time and skills bring meaningful opportunities to children across Karnataka.

How You Can Help

Volunteer Opportunities

Find an area that matches your passion, background, and schedule.

📚

Education Support

Facilitate Grantha Mitra reading circles, assist teachers during FLN hours, conduct remedial literacy games, and mentor small groups of primary school learners.

🎨

Creative Activities

Conduct drawing, craft, origami, theater, drama, and folk music workshops that spark imaginative thinking and emotional expression among young learners.

📊

Research & Data

Help transcribe field assessments, organize baseline-to-endline learning metrics, conduct literature reviews, and assist in program evaluation reports.

🎪

Events Coordination

Assist in planning and executing cluster Science Melas, Pustaka Habba reading festivals, community gatherings, and school exhibition logistics.

📸

Communication & Media

Capture classroom photography, document student success stories, design newsletters, and share our community impact across public communication channels.

🤝

Community Outreach

Engage with parents, school management committees (SDMC), and neighborhood elders to build community-driven enthusiasm for education.
